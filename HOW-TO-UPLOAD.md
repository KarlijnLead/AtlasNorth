# Putting the Atlas of the North online (GitHub Pages)

## Upload (about 5 minutes)
1. Sign in at github.com (create a free account if you don't have one).
2. Click **+ → New repository**. Name it, for example, `atlas-of-the-north`. Choose **Public** and click **Create repository**.
3. On the new page click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Open the unzipped `atlas-of-the-north` folder on your computer, select **everything inside it** (index.html and the media folder) and drag it into the browser window. Click **Commit changes**.
5. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Branch*, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
6. After a minute or two the address appears at the top of that page, for example `https://yourname.github.io/atlas-of-the-north/`.

## Password
- Leaders open the address and enter the team password.
- "Remember me on this device" is ticked by default, so they only type it once per device.
- The text of the site is encrypted inside index.html. Without the password it cannot be read, even though the repository is public.
- Photos and videos in the media folder are not encrypted; they contain no internal information.

## Changing the password or the content
Ask Claude for a new package with the new password or changes, then upload the new index.html over the old one (Add file → Upload files).
